## Keywords:

### ARO(Annualized Rate of Occurrence)

```text
- How often Is this going to happen / Likelihood of this happening.
```

### AV(Asset Value)

```text
- The value of the asset to the organization
- Includes the cost of the asset,effect on company sales, potential regulatory fines,etc
```

### EF(Exposure Factor)

```text
- the percentage of the value lost due to an incident.
- Losing a quarter of the value is .25
- Losing the entire asset is 1.0
```

### SLE(Single Loss Expectancy)

```text
- What is the monetary loss if a single event occurs?
- Asset Value(AV) x Exposure factor (EF)

Eg; Laptop stolen = $1000(AV) x 1.0(EF) = $1000(SLE)
```

### ALE (Annualized Loss Expectancy)

```text
- Annualized Rate of Occurrence(ARO) X SLE

so basically SLE but multiplied by the number of assets affected per year.
```

```text
The business impact can be more than monetary.
    - Quantitative vs qualitative.
```

## Likelihood and probability

### Risk likelihood

```text
- Qualitative measurement of risk
- Rare , possible , almost certain,etc.
```

### Risk Probability

```text
- A quantitative measurement of risk
- A statistical measurement
- Can be based on historical performance
```

```text

```

## Risk appetite and tolerance

### Risk appetite

```text
- A broad description of risk-taking deemed acceptable.
- The amount of accepted risk before taking any action to
  reduce that risk.
```

### Risk appetite posture

```text
- Qualitative description for readiness to take risk
- Conservative,neutral and expansionary
```

### Risk tolerance

```text
- An acceptable variance(usually larger) from the risk appetite.
```

```text
eg: 55 speed limit

    risk appetite: speed limit is how much risk you're willing to take, so 55 is the set
    amount of risk when driving

    posture: The readiness to take said risk.

    tolerance: Driver's willingness to violate the speed limit
```

## Risk Management Strategies

### Transfer

```text
- move the risk to another party
- Buy some Cybersecurity insurance
```

### Accept

```text
- A business decision; we'll take the risk!
This is often the usually the course.
```

### Accept with exemption

```text
- A security policy or regulation cannot be followed

- May be based on available security controls,size of the organization,total assets,etc.
- Exemption may need approval.
```

### Accept with exemption

```text
- Internal security policies are not applied
- Monthly security updated must be applied within 3 days.
- the monthly updates cause a critical software package to crash
- An exception is made to the update time frame

- basically some patches might break shit so they delay it.
```

### Avoid

```text
- We avoid the high risk activity.
- This effectively removes the risk.
```

### Mitigate

```text
- We try to decrease the risk level in some way
```


---

## (MTTR) Mean time to repair -

```text
- Average time required to fix an issue
- The includes the time spent diagnosing the problem
- An important metric for determining the cost and time associated with unplanned
  outages.
```

---

## (MTBF) Mean time between failures -

```text
- The time between outages
- Can be used as a prediction or calculated based on historical performance.
- Total uptime / number of break downs
  statistically plan for possible outages.
```
