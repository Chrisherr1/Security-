# 5.3-Third-party risk

```text
- Every Organization works with vendors.
- Payroll,customer relationship management,
  email marketing,travel,raw materials.

- Important company data is often shared
    - May be required for cloud-based services

- Perform a risk assessment
    - Categorize risk by vendor and manage the risk

- Use contracts for clear understanding
    - Make sure everyone understands the expectations
    - Use the contract to enforce a secure environment
```

---

## Penetration Testing

### Pentest

```text
    - Simulate an attack
- Similar to vulnerability scanning
    - Except we actually try to exploit the vulnerabilities
- Often a compliance mandate
    - May include a legal requirement
- May be a 3rd party
    - Very specialized
```

---

## Rules of Engagement

```text
- Defines the scope and purpose of the pentest

- Type of testing and schedule
- On site physical breach,internal test,external test,
- Normal working hours only? or not?

- Usually include the rules and
  an IP address range and how to handle sensitive information and what is in scope and
  what is out of scope.
```

---

## Right-To audit clauses

```text
- Common to work with business partners
    - May have to do data sharing or outsourcing

- Third-party providers
    - can hold all of the data Manage Internet access
    - Are they themselves secure?

Right to audit should in the contract
    - A legal agreement to have the option to perform a security audit at any time.
    - Everyone agrees to the terms and conditions
    - Ability to verify security before a breach occurs
```

---

## Evidence of internal audits

```text
- these are meant to evaluate the effectiveness of security controls
    - have a third part perform an audit
- May be required for compliance
    - Its a good idea even without industry standards
- Check for security controls and processes
    - Access management, off boarding, password security, VPN controls,etc.
    - There's always a way to improve.
- Good idea to perform at a reasonable frequency
    - A single audit isn't helpful in the long-term.
```

---

## Supply chain analysis

```text
- Understand how we get a product from the vendor to the customer
- Understand how security is in both you and the vendor.
- Document everything
```

---

## Independent assessments

```text
- bring in a smart person or team to evaluate security and provide recommendation

- consultants basically
```

---

## Vendor Selection

```text
Be careful who you make your vendor.
Check the company and financial status.

See if they have a conflict of interest. What if they work for a bigger competitor they
might keep stock from you or if they are family of the CFO.
```

---

## Vendor Monitoring

```text
Ongoing management of the vendor relationship is your job.

Reviews should occur on a regular basis.

Vendors should be checked based on different indicators.

Assign a person to be in charge of the vendor relationship, it will be their job to make
sure they are doing what they are suppose to be doing.
```

# 5.3 Agreement Types

## SLA - Service Level Agreement

```text
- This is the minimum terms for services provided.
- Uptime, response time agreement, etc.
- Commonly used between customers and service providers.
```

> **If you're starting a partnership..**

---

## MOU - Memorandum of Understanding(MOU)

```text
- Both sides agree in general to the contents of the memorandum
- Usually states common goals but not much more
- May include statements of confidentiality
- Informal letter of intent; not a signed contract

- Just a theory of how these 2 might form a partnership, not anything official.
```

> **If it gets serious..**

---

## MOA - Memorandum of Agreement (MOA)

```text
- The next step above a MOU
- Both sides conditionally agree to the objectives
- Can also be a legal document, even without legal language
- Unlike a contract, may not contain legally enforceable promises.
```

> **If you're going to have an ongoing partnership with a company..**

---

## MSA - Master Service Agreement(MSA)

```text
- Legal Contract and agreement of terms
- A broad framework to cover later transactions
- Many detailed negotiations happen here
- Future projects will be based on this agreement
```

---

## Work order(WO)/ Statement of Work(SOW)

```text
- Details the scope of the job,location,deliverables schedule,acceptance criteria, and
  more.
- Used in conjunction with a MSA
- Was the job done correctly? refer to the SOW
```

> **If you need to keep things confidential**

---

## NDA - Non-disclosure agreement

```text
- Confidentiality agreement between parties
    - Information in the agreement should be not discloses
- Protects confidential information
    - Trade secrets
    - business activities
- Unilateral or bilateral
    - One-way NDA or mutual NDA
- Formal contract
    - usually requiring a signature.
```

> **If you're entering a formal partnership with a party..**

---

## BPA - Business Partners Agreement

```text
- Going to business together
- Owner stake
- Financial contract

It will also describe how the business will work with this partnership in place.

eg: decision making, who makes the decision, BPA lists specific individuals and scope.
```
