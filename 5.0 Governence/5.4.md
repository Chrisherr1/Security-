# Compliance

```text
The process of laws, policies, and regulations.

There are often penalties if you're not in compliance.
This includes fines,loss of employment, or even jail time.
```

---

## Compliance Reporting

```text
- Internal
    - Monitor and report on organizational compliance efforts
    - Large organizations have a central compliance officer (CCO)
    - Also used to provide details to customers or potential investors.

- External
    - Documentation required by external or industry regulators
    - May require annual or ongoing reporting
    - Missing or invalid reporting could result in fines and/or
      sanctions.
```

---

## Common Regulatory Compliance

```text
SOX - Sarbanes-Oxley Act
    - The public company accounting reform and investor protection act of 2002

HIPAA - The health insurance Portability and Accountability Act
    - Extensive Healthcare standards for storage,use,and transmission of healthcare
      information.

GLBA - The Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act of 1999
    - Disclosure of privacy information from financial institutions.
```

---

## Penalties for not following

```text
HIPAA - Fine up to 50,0000 or 1 year prison.(Felony)
    - If under false pretenses:
        5 years prison, 100,000 fine
    - if intent to sell,transfer or use identifiable information for commercial use
        - 10 years prison,250,000 fine
```

### Loss of License

## European union regulation

```text
- Data protection and privacy for individuals in the EU.
- Name, Address, photo, email address details, posts on social networking
  websites,medical information, a computer's IP address,etc.

Controls export of personal data, users can decide where their data goes and can request
to remove the data from search engines.

This allows people to control their personal data and gives them the right to be
forgotten.
```

---

## Data Subject

```text
- Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
    - An individual with personal data

    this includes everyone's:
        name,ID,number,address information, genetic makeup, physical characteristics,
        location data,etc.
```

---

## Laws and regulations

```text
- Privacy is ideally defined from the perspective of the data subject.
```

---

## Data responsibilities

```text
- High-level data relationships
    - Organizational responsibilities, not always technical

- Data owner
    - Accountable for specific data, often senior officer

- Data Controller
    - Manages the purposes and means by which personal data is processed

- Data Processor
    - Often a third-party or different group.
```

### Payroll controller and processor example

```text
Payroll department is the controller and a third party who processes the payroll and
stores the employee information is the processor.
```
