-----------------
Denial of Service
-----------------
    - Force a service to fail, by overloading the service.
    - Taking advantage of design failure or vulnerability
    - cause a system to be unavailable
    - Create a smoke screen from some other exploit
        - usually a precursor to a DNS spoofing attack
    
    A 'friendly' DoS
    ----------------
        - unintential DoS
            - Network  Layer 2 loop without STP
            - Bandwidth DoS - donwloading multi gigbyte linux distros over a DSL line.
            - water line breaks
    
    Distributed denial of service(DDoS)
    --------------------------------
        - launch an army of computers to break down a service
            - Use all the badwidth or reources
        - This is why they have botnets
            - Thousands to millions of computers at your command
        - typically a Symetric attack

    DDoS reflection and amplification
    --------------------------------
        - Turn a small attack into a big attack
        - Uses protocols with little authentication
            - NTP,DNS,ICMP
            - Common example of protocol abuse
