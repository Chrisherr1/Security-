Hardware Vulnerabilities
----------------------------------
    - Many hardware devices dont allow you to get access to operating system.
    - Meaning that updates are very important, cuz you just dont know

    Firmware -
        - The software inside of the hardware
        - Vendors are the only ones who can fix their hardware

        -Vendors can be security lax at times.

    End-of-life Notice -
        - Sometimes vendors/manufactuers will tell you when they plan 
        on stop selling a product or stop supporting the product.
        
        - If they stop that could mean that your system will become 
        vulnerable to attack due to the lack of patches/updates
    
    End of service life(EOSL) -
        - Once a manufactuers stop selling a products && support
        is no longer available for the product

        - No ongoing security patches or updates
        - May have a premium-cost support option

        - IF IT HITS THIS _ REPLACE ASAP

    Legacy platforms -
        - Some devices remain installed for a long time
        - Legacy devices
            - Older Operating systems,applications,middleware
        - May be running end-of-life software
        - May require additional security protections
            - additional firewall rules
            - IPS signitures for older operating systems
