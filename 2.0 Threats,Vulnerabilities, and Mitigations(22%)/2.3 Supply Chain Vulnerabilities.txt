------------------------------
Supply Chain Vulnerabilities
------------------------------

    Supply Chain risk

        The chain from when your products get made from all the way to
        product delivery. 

        - Attacker can infect any step along the way.
        - People trust their suppliers.
        - One exploit can infect the entire chain.

        - Service Providers
            - You can always control a service provider
            - many Types
                network,utility,offic cleaning,payroll,accounting,cloud services,system admin

            - consider ongoing security audits of all providers
                - should be included with the contract
            
        - Hardware providers
            - Can you trust your new device? (server,router,switch)
            - use a small supplier base
                - tight control of vendors
            - strict controls over policies and procedures
            - Security should be part of the overall design

            -cisco or not cisco?
                - all traffic goes though SWITCHES and ROUTERS
                - sometimes are counterfiet so double check its acutally 
                legit
        
        - Software providers
            - initial installation
                - Digital signatures during installation
            - updates and patches
                - automatic so make sure its legit 
            - Open source is not immune
                - Sometimes compromised
